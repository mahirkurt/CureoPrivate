# brand-launch v1.0 — Launch & Governance Protocol

## Raison d'être

Excellent strategy + identity + touchpoints fails if launch is mishandled. Most rebrands suffer from launch fragility — internal stakeholders don't know the new positioning, external campaigns roll out before employees can answer questions, brand guidelines arrive as a 80-page PDF nobody reads, trademark + domain gaps surface during launch week.

This skill produces a complete launch plan synthesizing all upstream work into operational rollout — internal-first per Rodriguez doctrine, externally sequenced per Wheeler Phase 5, with brand governance infrastructure (champions, brand center, guidelines, training) in place before external visibility begins.

The final skill in the brand ecosystem chain. Consumes all upstream handoffs.

### Step 1 — Launch Context Triage

Identify launch context from upstream + brief:

| Context | Distinctive emphasis |
|---|---|
| **Greenfield launch** | New brand entering market; emphasis on awareness building, brand-codes establishment |
| **Rebrand launch** | Existing customers migrating; emphasis on communication continuity, equity preservation, employee retraining |
| **Sub-brand launch** | New entity within parent system; emphasis on architecture clarity, parent-sub linkage |
| **M&A integration launch** | Two brands becoming one; emphasis on dual-employee alignment, customer continuity, asset rationalization |
| **Refresh launch** | Visual evolution without strategic change; emphasis on continuity messaging, low-disruption rollout |
| **Repositioning launch** | Strategic shift without identity replacement; emphasis on narrative shift, expectation reset |

Each context activates different reference emphases.

### Step 2 — Trademark, Domain, Social Handle Securing

The legal + digital foundation. Reference `trademark-domain-social-checklist.md`. Components:

#### Trademark filings
- File jurisdictions: USPTO (US), EUIPO (EU), UKIPO (UK), national offices for primary markets, Madrid Protocol for multi-jurisdictional efficiency
- Classes: Nice Classification — identify operating + adjacent classes
- Filing timing: should precede external launch by 3-6 months minimum
- Trademark monitoring: ongoing watch service for infringement
- Common law use evidence collection (USPTO requires "intent-to-use" or evidence)

#### Domain registration
- Primary domain (preferred TLD)
- Common TLD variants (.com, .net, country code TLDs in target markets)
- Common misspellings + defensive registrations
- Industry-specific TLDs if relevant (.studio, .design, .technology, .law)
- DNS configuration + email setup
- Domain renewal calendar (multi-year renewals preferred to prevent lapses)

#### Social handle securing
- Major platforms: Instagram, LinkedIn, X/Twitter, Facebook, TikTok, YouTube, Pinterest
- Newer platforms: Threads, Bluesky, Mastodon (sector-dependent)
- B2B-specific: GitHub (if technology), Behance/Dribbble (if creative), Glassdoor (claim employer page)
- Handle consistency across platforms (same handle on all, or close variants where unavailable)
- Account verification (LinkedIn Company, X Verified Org, etc.)

### Step 3 — Internal-First Launch Plan

Reference `imc-internal-first-diffusion.md`. The internal launch must precede external launch.

#### Internal launch timeline (typical)

| Week | Milestone |
|---|---|
| -8 | Leadership briefing complete; senior team can articulate the brand |
| -6 | Manager cascade complete; all managers briefed |
| -4 | All-hands brand introduction; full team encounters BrandScript + identity |
| -3 | Department workshops; team-level engagement |
| -2 | Champion identification + initial training |
| -1 | Final internal artifact distribution (employee handbook updates, onboarding revision, internal portal content) |
| 0 | External launch begins |

#### Internal launch components

**Leadership engagement** (per `brand-champions-program.md`):
- Leadership retreat or workshop series
- Q&A protocol for handling employee questions
- Leadership-as-storyteller training

**Manager cascade**:
- Manager toolkit (talking points, FAQ, slide deck, video assets)
- Cascade timeline and tracking
- Feedback collection mechanism

**All-hands event**:
- Format (in-person, virtual, hybrid)
- Content arc (the why → the what → the how → the next steps)
- Founder / CEO speaking role
- Q&A session
- Take-home artifact (brand book, summary card, exclusive merchandise)

**Department workshops**:
- Tailored content per department (sales, product, marketing, customer service, operations)
- Each workshop reframes the brand from that department's perspective
- Workshop owners + facilitators

### Step 4 — Brand Champion Program Design

Reference `brand-champions-program.md`. Champions are the brand's internal evangelists who continue spreading the story after the launch event.

#### Champion identification criteria
- Customer-facing role with brand contact frequency
- Demonstrated affinity for the brand (existing internal advocacy)
- Storytelling aptitude
- Sphere of influence (internal + external)
- Cross-department representation

#### Champion program structure
- Selection ratio: 5-15% of employees
- Initial training: 2-day intensive or 4 half-day workshops
- Refresher cadence: quarterly
- Champion recognition: badge, role description addition, public acknowledgment
- Champion responsibilities: explicit + voluntary
- Champion network: dedicated Slack/Teams channel, monthly call
- Champion contribution: feedback to brand team, story collection, peer education

### Step 5 — Brand Center / Online Asset Library Specification

Reference `brand-center-spec.md`. The brand center is the digital infrastructure for ongoing brand governance.

#### Brand center user groups
- Internal employees (all departments)
- External partners (agencies, vendors, contractors)
- Press (high-resolution assets, fact sheets)
- Customers (sometimes, for co-branding)

#### Brand center content
- Brand platform document (executive summary access)
- Brand story / BrandScript
- Visual identity assets (logos, color, typography downloads)
- Touchpoint specifications + templates
- Brand guidelines (full document + searchable web version)
- Photography library
- Illustration library
- Icon library
- Video assets
- Audio assets (sonic logo, music beds)
- Brand voice + tone reference
- Approval workflow tools
- Asset request forms
- Brand news / updates

#### Brand center governance
- Model: cop (locked, approval-required) / hybrid (some assets self-serve) / concierge (most assets self-serve with guidelines)
- Access tiering by user group
- Asset versioning + retiring protocol
- Update cadence
- Owner role + budget

### Step 6 — Brand Guidelines Document

Reference `brand-guidelines-anatomy.md`. The brand guidelines document is the canonical reference for how to use the brand.

#### Brand guidelines structure (recommended)

```
Section 1: Brand foundation
  1.1 Vision, mission, values
  1.2 Positioning
  1.3 Brand personality
  1.4 Brand voice + tone

Section 2: Verbal identity
  2.1 Brand name + variants
  2.2 Tagline + use rules
  2.3 Voice principles
  2.4 Writing examples + anti-examples

Section 3: Visual identity
  3.1 Logo + lockup rules
  3.2 Color palette + applications
  3.3 Typography system
  3.4 Iconography
  3.5 Photography style
  3.6 Illustration system
  3.7 Motion language
  3.8 Sonic identity
  3.9 Adaptive identity rules (if Route 4 was selected)

Section 4: Application
  4.1 Stationery
  4.2 Signage + wayfinding
  4.3 Packaging
  4.4 Environments
  4.5 Digital touchpoints
  4.6 Uniform / dress code
  4.7 Ephemera + merchandise
  4.8 Co-branding rules

Section 5: Governance
  5.1 Approval workflow
  5.2 Asset request process
  5.3 Brand center access
  5.4 Champion network
  5.5 Update / refresh protocol
  5.6 Brand legal (trademark uses)
```

Most brands today produce brand guidelines as both PDF (for download/print) and web format (for search + interactive components). The web format wins; the PDF is reference fallback.

### Step 7 — Employee Onboarding + eLearning Brief

Reference `employee-onboarding-elearning.md`. Brand integration into hiring + onboarding.

#### New hire onboarding
- Day 1 brand introduction (BrandScript presentation, 60 min)
- Week 1 brand immersion (guidelines tour, brand center walkthrough, champion meet)
- 30-day brand proficiency check (can the new hire articulate the brand?)
- 90-day brand contribution (new hire submits a brand story or observation)

#### eLearning module
- Asynchronous brand orientation (15-30 min self-paced)
- Includes: brand story, voice + tone training, asset usage guidance, common pitfalls
- Integrated into HR / LMS infrastructure
- Updated when brand evolves

### Step 8 — External Launch Plan

Reference `imc-external-diffusion.md`. After internal saturation.

#### External launch sequencing
- Pre-launch teaser phase (optional, brand-dependent)
- Launch moment (the day / week of public reveal)
- Post-launch sustainment (4-12 weeks of structured rollout)
- Integration into ongoing marketing rhythm

#### External launch channels
- Owned: website refresh, email to customer base, blog/content
- Earned: PR strategy, journalist briefings, analyst briefings (B2B)
- Paid: advertising launch campaign, paid social
- Event-based: launch event, conference reveal, retail experience

#### Customer migration (rebrand context)
- Existing customer notification timing
- Customer-facing FAQ
- Account-management touchpoint for high-value customers
- Visual asset migration (account portals, app interfaces, packaging in distribution)

### Step 9 — KPI Dashboard

Brand performance measurement. Reference `kpi-dashboard.md`. Standard categories:

#### Awareness metrics
- Aided brand recall (% of target segment recognizing brand name)
- Unaided brand recall (% of target segment naming brand unprompted in category)
- Brand search volume trend
- Direct website traffic trend

#### Consideration metrics
- Brand consideration set inclusion (% considering brand for purchase decisions)
- Demo/quote/contact request volume
- Sales-qualified lead volume
- Pipeline source attribution

#### Preference metrics
- First-choice preference rate vs competitors
- Net Promoter Score (NPS)
- Customer satisfaction (CSAT)
- Win rate vs primary competitors

#### Advocacy metrics
- Customer referral rate
- Customer-generated content volume
- Online review sentiment
- Brand mention sentiment
- Champion network growth

#### Measurement cadence
- Weekly: web traffic, search volume, social engagement
- Monthly: pipeline metrics, NPS sample
- Quarterly: brand tracking study (formal awareness/consideration/preference research)
- Annual: comprehensive brand health assessment

## Known Limits

- Cannot execute the launch — produces the plan; execution is operational/agency work.
- Cannot file trademarks or register domains — produces the checklist; legal/admin execution is external.
- Cannot build the brand center — produces the specification; web/IT build is external.
- Cannot measure brand performance directly — defines the KPI dashboard; instrumentation is external.
- Cannot guarantee launch success; can substantially reduce risk through complete plan + internal-first sequencing.
