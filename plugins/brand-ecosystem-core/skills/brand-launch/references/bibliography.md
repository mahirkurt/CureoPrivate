## Skill-Specific Deep Cuts

### Schultz, Don E., Stanley I. Tannenbaum, and Robert F. Lauterborn. *Integrated Marketing Communications*. NTC Business Books, 1993.

Foundational IMC text. While Rodriguez supersedes operationally, Schultz et al. provide the theoretical justification for integrated launch sequencing. Used as conceptual backbone reference rather than tactical guide.

Used in: `references/imc-external-diffusion.md` (theoretical context section)

Citation: `[Schultz et al., IMC Foundation]`

### Davis, Scott M. *Brand Asset Management*. 2nd edition. Jossey-Bass, 2002.

Asset management implications for brand center build. Davis asset taxonomy informs brand-center-spec.md content architecture.

Used in: `references/brand-center-spec.md`

Citation: `[Davis, Asset Taxonomy]`

## Citation Discipline Rule

Every launch sequencing decision, KPI definition, governance assignment, and brand center specification must carry inline citation. Operational decisions without source backing are downgraded.

When citing IMC frameworks, prefer Rodriguez as operational source. Schultz et al. citation is appropriate only when invoking foundational IMC theory itself.
