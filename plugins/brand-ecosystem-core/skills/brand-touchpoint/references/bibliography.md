## Skill-Specific Deep Cuts

### Mollerup, Per. *Marks of Excellence: The History and Taxonomy of Trademarks*. Phaidon, 2013.

Signage design taxonomy and historical case studies. Mollerup's classification for monument signage, wayfinding, and architectural integration drives signage-environment.md.

Used in: `references/signage-environment.md`

Citation: `[Mollerup, Signage]`

### Costa, Joan. *Designing Brand Identity Materials*. Wolfgang Weingart Press, 2008.

Stationery and business papers specifications methodology. Stock weight, paper finish, and printing technique recommendations inform stationery-business-papers.md.

Used in: `references/stationery-business-papers.md`

Citation: `[Costa, Stationery]`

### Klimchuk, Marianne Rosner, and Sandra A. Krasovec. *Packaging Design: Successful Product Branding from Concept to Shelf*. 3rd edition. Wiley, 2019.

Packaging structural and graphic design methodology. Three-layer framework (structural + graphic + sensorial) directly informs packaging-structural-graphic.md.

Used in: `references/packaging-structural-graphic.md`

Citation: `[Klimchuk & Krasovec, Three-Layer]`

### Lawson, Bryan. *Design in Mind*. Architectural Press, 1994.

Environmental design cognition — how spatial arrangements shape brand experience. "Design as conversation between intention and inhabitant" principle.

Used in: `references/retail-environment.md`

Citation: `[Lawson, Conversation]`

## Citation Discipline Rule

Every per-touchpoint specification in the output document must carry at least one inline citation tying its methodology to a canonical source. Specifications without citation are treated as proprietary opinion and downgraded.
