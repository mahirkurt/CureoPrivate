## Skill-Specific Deep Cuts

### Krishna, Aradhna. *Customer Sense: How the 5 Senses Influence Buying Behavior*. Palgrave Macmillan, 2013.

Sensory marketing fundamentals — five-sense atmospheric specification methodology.

Used in: `references/sensory-neuromarketing.md`

Citation: `[Krishna, Customer Sense]`

### Lindstrom, Martin. *Brand Sense: Sensory Secrets Behind the Stuff We Buy*. Free Press, 2010 (revised).

Multi-sensory branding case studies — sonic logo cases (Intel, Nokia, MGM), scent strategy cases (luxury retail), tactile case studies.

Used in: `references/sensory-neuromarketing.md`

Citation: `[Lindstrom, Brand Sense]`

### Hyndman, Sarah. *Why Fonts Matter*. Virgin Books, 2016.

Typography psychology for sensory-aware visual decisions. Used as supporting reference for atmosphere-aware typeface selection in multi-sensory contexts.

Citation: `[Hyndman, Fonts]`

### Wedel, Michel and Rik Pieters. *Visual Marketing*. Lawrence Erlbaum, 2008.

Visual attention research informing the favicon-to-billboard scale specimens (16px legibility vs 30m impact analysis).

Used in: `references/touchpoint-atmosphere-system.md` (scale specimens section)

Citation: `[Wedel & Pieters, Visual Attention]`

## Citation Discipline Rule

Every visual identity decision, scale specimen specification, sensory layer brief, and asset management rule must carry inline citation. Decisions without source backing are downgraded.
