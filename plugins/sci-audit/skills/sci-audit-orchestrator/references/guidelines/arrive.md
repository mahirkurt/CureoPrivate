# ARRIVE 2.0 — animal (in vivo) research

The "Essential 10" plus recommended items. Report present / missing / partial
with locating text. Full statement: Percie du Sert N et al. PLoS Biol 2020;18:e3000410.

Essential 10:
1. **Study design** — groups incl. control; the experimental unit.
2. **Sample size** — total number of animals; how it was decided (power analysis).
3. **Inclusion/exclusion criteria** — pre-defined criteria; any animals/data
   excluded and why; analysis at the level of the experimental unit.
4. **Randomisation** — whether/how animals were allocated to groups; order of
   treatment/assessment randomised.
5. **Blinding** — who was blinded, when (allocation, conduct, outcome
   assessment, analysis).
6. **Outcome measures** — defined primary/secondary outcomes.
7. **Statistical methods** — details incl. the unit of analysis; assumptions.
8. **Experimental animals** — species, strain, sex, age/weight, source.
9. **Experimental procedures** — what, how, when, where — enough to replicate.
10. **Results** — for each outcome, a summary with a measure of variability +
    effect size and CI.

Recommended: ethics approval; housing/husbandry; humane endpoints; interpretation/
generalisability to human biology; protocol registration; data access; conflicts;
funding.

Red flags for LLM text: no sample-size justification; no randomisation/blinding;
missing species/strain/sex; results with no variability or effect size;
generalising animal findings to humans without caveat.
