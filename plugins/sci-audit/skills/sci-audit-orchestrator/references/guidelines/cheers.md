# CHEERS 2022 — health economic evaluations

Check each item; report present / missing / partial with locating text. Full
statement: Husereau D et al. BMJ 2022;376:e067975.

- **Title/Abstract** — identifies the study as an economic evaluation and the
  interventions compared; structured abstract.
- **Introduction** — background and objectives incl. the decision problem.
- **Methods** — health economic analysis plan; target population + subgroups;
  setting and location; comparators + rationale; perspective; time horizon;
  discount rate(s); selection/measurement/valuation of outcomes (e.g. QALYs);
  measurement/valuation of costs + currency and price year; currency conversion;
  the analytic model (structure, assumptions) with justification; analytics and
  assumptions; characterising heterogeneity; characterising distributional
  effects; characterising uncertainty (sensitivity / probabilistic analysis).
- **Results** — study parameters (values, ranges, distributions, sources);
  summary incremental costs and outcomes (e.g. ICER); effect of uncertainty.
- **Discussion** — findings, limitations, generalisability, current knowledge.
- **Other** — source of funding; conflicts of interest.

Red flags for LLM text: an ICER with no time horizon or perspective; costs with
no price year/currency; a model with no uncertainty analysis.
