# COREQ — qualitative research (interviews & focus groups)

32-item checklist across three domains. Report present / missing / partial with
locating text. Full statement: Tong A et al. Int J Qual Health Care 2007;19:349.

- **Domain 1 — Research team & reflexivity:** interviewer identity; credentials;
  occupation; gender; experience/training; relationship established with
  participants prior to study; participant knowledge of the interviewer;
  interviewer characteristics / assumptions (reflexivity / positionality).
- **Domain 2 — Study design:** methodological orientation & theory (grounded
  theory, phenomenology, thematic analysis…); sampling method; approach & size;
  non-participation; setting of data collection; presence of non-participants;
  description of sample; interview guide + pilot; repeat interviews;
  audio/visual recording; field notes; duration; data saturation / information
  power; transcripts returned to participants.
- **Domain 3 — Analysis & findings:** number of coders; coding-tree description;
  themes derived inductively vs in advance; software; participant checking (member
  checking); quotations presented + participant identified; data–findings
  consistency; clarity of major and minor themes.

Red flags for LLM text: "themes emerged" with no coding process; claims of
"saturation" with no definition of how it was judged; quotations with no
participant identifier; positivist reliability language (inter-coder kappa) on a
reflexive thematic analysis without framing it as such.
