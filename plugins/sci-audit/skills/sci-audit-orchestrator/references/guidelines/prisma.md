# PRISMA 2020 — systematic reviews & meta-analyses

Check the manuscript for each item; report present / missing / partial with the
locating text. Full statement: Page MJ et al. BMJ 2021;372:n71.

- **Title** — identifies the report as a systematic review.
- **Abstract** — structured summary (background, methods, results, conclusions, registration).
- **Introduction (3–4)** — rationale; objectives with a PICO-style question.
- **Methods (5–15)** — eligibility criteria; information sources + last search date;
  full search strategy for at least one database; selection process; data-collection
  process; data items; risk-of-bias assessment; effect measures; synthesis methods;
  reporting-bias assessment; certainty assessment (e.g. GRADE).
- **Results (16–22)** — study selection with a flow diagram (records identified →
  screened → included, with exclusion reasons); study characteristics; risk of bias;
  results of individual studies; results of syntheses; reporting biases; certainty.
- **Discussion (23)** — interpretation; limitations; implications.
- **Other (24–27)** — registration & protocol; support/funding; competing interests;
  availability of data, code, and materials.

Red flags for LLM text: a flow diagram with numbers that do not reconcile; a
"comprehensive search" with no databases or date named; claims of "no
heterogeneity" with no I² reported.
