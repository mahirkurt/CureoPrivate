# SPIRIT 2013 — clinical trial protocols

Check each item; report present / missing / partial with locating text. Full
statement: Chan A-W et al. Ann Intern Med 2013;158:200. (Protocols, not reports —
use CONSORT for the completed trial.)

- **Administrative** — descriptive title identifying design/population/
  intervention; trial registration + data set; protocol version + date;
  funding; roles and responsibilities of sponsor/authors.
- **Introduction** — background and rationale incl. why a trial is needed;
  objectives / hypotheses.
- **Methods** — trial design (type, allocation ratio, framework:
  superiority/equivalence/non-inferiority); study setting; eligibility criteria;
  interventions with sufficient detail to replicate + modifications/adherence;
  pre-specified primary and secondary outcomes with measurement time points;
  participant timeline; sample size with assumptions; recruitment strategy.
- **Methods: assignment** — sequence generation; allocation-concealment
  mechanism; implementation; blinding + emergency unblinding.
- **Methods: data** — data collection, management, and statistical methods incl.
  handling of missing data; interim analyses + stopping rules.
- **Ethics & dissemination** — research ethics approval; consent; confidentiality;
  declaration of interests; access to data; ancillary/post-trial care;
  dissemination policy incl. publication.

Red flags for LLM text: a protocol with no registration; outcomes with no
measurement time; sample size with no assumptions; missing consent/ethics.
