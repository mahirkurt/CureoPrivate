# SRQR — Standards for Reporting Qualitative Research

21 items. Report present / missing / partial with locating text. Full
statement: O'Brien BC et al. Acad Med 2014;89:1245. Use SRQR when the design is
qualitative but not interview/focus-group-specific (COREQ is narrower).

- **Title & abstract** — identifies the study as qualitative; summarises key elements.
- **Introduction** — problem formulation; purpose / research question.
- **Methods** — qualitative approach & research paradigm; researcher
  characteristics & reflexivity; context; sampling strategy; ethical issues
  (approval, consent, confidentiality); data-collection methods + instruments;
  units of study (participants/sites); data-processing; data analysis; techniques
  to enhance trustworthiness (triangulation, audit trail, member checking).
- **Results/findings** — findings synthesised with prior scholarship; evidence
  (quotes, field notes) linked to interpretation.
- **Discussion** — integration with prior work, implications, transferability;
  limitations.
- **Other** — conflicts of interest; funding.

Red flags for LLM text: no reflexivity/positionality statement; trustworthiness
claimed with no technique named; findings with no supporting evidence excerpts.
